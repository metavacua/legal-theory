Appendix: A Thematic Compendium of Landmark State Supreme Court Decisions

Case Name & Citation State Year Legal Domain Summary of Significance
**State Constitutional Law**
*In re Quinlan*, 70 N.J. 10 NJ 1976 Privacy / Right to Die Established a patient's right to refuse life-sustaining medical treatment based on the state constitutional right to privacy, exercisable by a guardian for an incompetent patient. Pioneered the use of hospital ethics committees.
*Goodridge v. Dept. of Public Health*, 440 Mass. 309 MA 2003 Equality / Same-Sex Marriage First decision by a state's highest court legalizing same-sex marriage, finding the ban violated the liberty and equal protection guarantees of the state constitution.
*Baker v. State*, 170 Vt. 194 VT 1999 Equality / Same-Sex Marriage Held that denying same-sex couples the benefits of marriage violated the state constitution's "Common Benefits Clause," leading to the nation's first civil union law.
*Abbott v. Burke (II)*, 119 N.J. 287 NJ 1990 Education Ordered funding parity for poor urban school districts and mandated supplemental programs, including preschool, to ensure a "thorough and efficient" education under the state constitution.
*Rose v. Council for Better Education*, 790 S.W.2d 186 KY 1989 Education Declared the state's entire public school system unconstitutional and famously defined seven core capacities required for an "adequate" education, setting a benchmark for school finance litigation nationwide.
*Serrano v. Priest*, 5 Cal.3d 584 CA 1971 Education / Equality Held that California's property-tax-based school funding system violated the state's equal protection clause by making educational quality a function of district wealth.
*Held v. Montana* (No. CDV-2020-307, Mont. 1st Jud. Dist.) MT 2023 Environmental Rights First U.S. court ruling finding that a government violated constitutional rights based on climate change, holding that pro-fossil fuel policies violated the state constitution's guarantee of a "clean and healthful environment."
*Ravin v. State*, 537 P.2d 494 AK 1975 Privacy Held that the state constitution's explicit right to privacy protects an adult's possession of a small amount of marijuana for personal use in the home.
**Tort Law**
*MacPherson v. Buick Motor Co.*, 217 N.Y. 382 NY 1916 Product Liability Abolished the privity of contract requirement for negligence claims, holding that a manufacturer owes a duty of care to the ultimate consumer if a product is reasonably certain to be dangerous if negligently made.
*Palsgraf v. Long Island R.R. Co.*, 248 N.Y. 339 NY 1928 Negligence / Duty Established the principle that a duty of care is owed only to a foreseeable plaintiff within the "zone of danger," shaping modern legal analysis of proximate cause.
*Tarasoff v. Regents of Univ. of Cal.*, 17 Cal.3d 425 CA 1976 Duty to Protect Established that mental health professionals have a duty to take reasonable steps to protect a third party when a patient presents a credible threat of violence, creating an exception to therapist-patient confidentiality.
*Li v. Yellow Cab Co.*, 13 Cal.3d 804 CA 1975 Negligence Judicially abolished the "all-or-nothing" doctrine of contributory negligence and adopted a system of "pure" comparative negligence, apportioning liability based on fault.
*Sindell v. Abbott Laboratories*, 26 Cal.3d 588 CA 1980 Mass Torts / Liability Created the novel doctrine of "market share liability," allowing plaintiffs harmed by a fungible product (DES) who could not identify the specific manufacturer to sue multiple manufacturers, with liability apportioned by market share.
**Contract Law**
*Wood v. Lucy, Lady Duff-Gordon*, 222 N.Y. 88 NY 1917 Consideration Found consideration in an exclusive dealing contract by implying a promise of "reasonable efforts," thereby preventing the contract from being illusory.
*Jacob & Youngs, Inc. v. Kent*, 230 N.Y. 239 NY 1921 Performance / Damages Articulated the doctrine of "substantial performance," allowing recovery for a contract not perfectly performed, with damages measured by the difference in value rather than the cost of replacement for trivial defects.
*Hawkins v. McGee*, 84 N.H. 114 NH 1929 Damages Famously established the measure of expectation damages in contract law as the difference between the value of what was promised and what was delivered.
**Property Law**
*S. Burlington County NAACP v. Mount Laurel*, 67 N.J. 151 NJ 1975 Zoning / Land Use Established the "Mount Laurel Doctrine," requiring developing municipalities to use their zoning powers to provide a "fair share" of the regional need for affordable housing.
*Poletown Neigh. Council v. City of Detroit*, 410 Mich. 616 MI 1981 Eminent Domain Broadly interpreted "public use" to allow the condemnation of an entire neighborhood for transfer to a private corporation (General Motors) for economic development purposes. (Later overruled).
*Moore v. Regents of Univ. of Cal.*, 51 Cal.3d 120 CA 1990 Property / Torts Held that patients do not have a property right in their excised cells for a conversion claim, but that doctors have a fiduciary duty to disclose research and economic interests in a patient's biological materials.
*Boomer v. Atlantic Cement Co.*, 26 N.Y.2d 219 NY 1970 Nuisance / Remedies Refused to enjoin a nuisance where the economic harm of the injunction greatly outweighed the plaintiff's damages, instead awarding permanent damages and pioneering a "balancing of equities" approach.
*PASH v. Hawaii County Planning Comm'n*, 79 Haw. 424 HI 1995 Property / Customary Rights Held that traditional and customary gathering rights of native Hawaiians must be protected by state agencies in land use decisions, even on privately owned land.
**Criminal & Family Law**
*State v. Kelly*, 97 N.J. 178 NJ 1984 Criminal Law / Evidence Held that expert testimony on Battered Woman Syndrome is admissible to support a claim of self-defense, recognizing the need to explain the unique psychological state of abuse victims.
*In re Baby M*, 109 N.J. 396 NJ 1988 Family Law / Surrogacy Invalidated surrogacy-for-pay contracts as a violation of public policy against "baby-selling," while awarding custody based on the best interests of the child.
